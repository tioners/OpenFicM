## Design Context

### Users
Mobile novel writers working in short sessions on Android phones. They need to
write and review chapters locally, then use a configured remote model when an
AI assist action is useful.

### Brand Personality
Focused, calm, private. The interface should feel like a dependable writing
desk rather than a social feed or a marketing site.

### Aesthetic Direction
Refined utilitarian light UI with warm off-white surfaces, deep green actions,
and a restrained coral accent. Touch-first layouts target portrait Android
phones, including gesture navigation and long chapter lists.

### Design Principles
- Keep the editor and local data available without a server.
- Put frequent actions within 44dp touch targets and thumb reach.
- Show loading, error, and recovery states at every network or database boundary.
- Preserve drafts across navigation, keyboard use, and brief backgrounding.
